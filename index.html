ABOUT THE COMPANY

These are our Values

Technology

We use data to drive our Research and innovation for our products.

Love for the Environment

All our products are eco-friendly and designed with the lens of Sustainability. This adds less strain to the ecosystem and more impact to our customer's conscience.

Unit Economics

We understand Quality and Quantity drive supply and demand. So we ship our products at a lower unit price than our customers because of this fact. Vertical integration is key for us.

Circular Economy

Our products are made utilize facets of circular economy. We believe at the end of a life cycle for one product means, start of the life cycle of another product. Keeping the loop intact for no waste to accumulate.

Our Story

The company was started in 2019 with the aim of making sustainable technologies. In 2021, we were able to sucessfully incorporate our Organization. We have a close relationship with our customers and our target users. Our Customers want a closer bond to nature in their products. Thus, Chyau.Bio leans on nature for its inspiration. We use raw materials from nature, we employ biomaterials and bioorganisms as our primary workers and we manufacture sustainable, renewable and biodegradable solutions for agriculture and climate action. Our business model is based on leveraging the waste and the disgarded materials in the nature to provide our products to our customers at Unit Price. Since 2019, our team has been researching on cutting down costs in terms of production and maintaining consistency of our manufacturing process.
